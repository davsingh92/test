ABOUT ACTIVE HIRE SOLUTIONS

Active Hire Solutions is a recruitment and staffing consultancy focused on connecting organizations with suitable talent while helping candidates discover meaningful career opportunities.

WHO WE ARE

Recruitment Support Built Around People & Businesses

Active Hire Solutions provides recruitment and staffing support for organizations looking to build their teams and for candidates searching for suitable employment opportunities.

We work across IT, Non-IT and BPO recruitment requirements and support organizations with talent sourcing, candidate screening and recruitment coordination.

Our approach is focused on understanding the requirement, identifying relevant profiles and creating a structured connection between employers and candidates.

Our Focus

To make recruitment more structured, accessible and candidate-focused for both businesses and job seekers.
